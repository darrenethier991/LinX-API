About LinX

Linking People Together — Est. 2024

LinX was founded in Barrie, Ontario in 2024 by Darren Ethier with a simple idea: getting work done shouldn't depend on who you happen to know. Homeowners describe the job once; verified local contractors are matched and notified automatically.

The platform is automation-first and fully self-serve — from posting a job to contractor onboarding to billing — which is exactly what keeps it fast for homeowners and affordable for the trades. Built and operated right here in Simcoe County.
