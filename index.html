Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Is LinX really free for homeowners? +
Yes — completely. Homeowners can post projects, receive quotes, read reviews, and hire contractors without ever paying a cent. LinX earns revenue through contractor subscriptions and EchoForge commissions.
How are contractors verified? +
Every contractor on LinX goes through a profile review process including business info, trade type, and service area. We encourage contractors to collect and display real reviews from past clients — you can see exactly who you're hiring.
What trades does LinX cover? +
LinX supports all major residential trades — plumbers, electricians, roofers, landscapers, general contractors, HVAC techs, painters, flooring installers, and more. If it's a home service, there's a contractor for it on LinX.
What is EchoForge? +
EchoForge is our built-in automation marketplace. Contractors can buy, remix, and sell workflow automations — from lead nurture sequences to social media schedulers. It's a marketplace for contractor business tools, built right into your dashboard.
Can I cancel my subscription anytime? +
Yes. All plans are month-to-month (or annual with a 20% discount). You can cancel anytime from your profile settings, and you'll keep access through the end of your billing period.
What cities does LinX operate in? +
LinX is live across Canada with initial focus on Ontario cities including Toronto, Barrie, Ottawa, and surrounding areas. We're expanding rapidly — contractors in any province are welcome to join and capture early market advantage.
How does the URL shortener work? +
Contractor subscribers get access to LinX's built-in URL shortener. Create short links for any destination URL (your profile, a quote page, a promotion), share them on social media or in SMS campaigns, and track click analytics including geographic data, device breakdown, and more — all from your dashboard.
