Questions

Straight answers

Is there a free trial?

No. Your subscription activates the moment Stripe checkout completes, and leads start flowing immediately. Keeping LinX fully self-serve is what keeps plans affordable.

How do leads reach me?

Homeowner jobs are matched to your trades and service area automatically and appear in your dashboard the moment they're posted. Pro adds real-time SMS alerts so you can respond first.

Can I cancel or change plans?

Anytime, self-serve, from your Stripe billing portal — no calls, no emails, no retention scripts. Upgrades and downgrades take effect right away.

Do homeowners pay anything?

Never. Posting jobs and getting quotes is free for homeowners — contractor memberships are what keep the platform running.
